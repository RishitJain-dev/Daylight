# **Daylight**
An alarm clock with a TFT 2.25 inch screen and dial to act as not just a alarm clock, but also as a timer, stopwatch and pomodoro focus timer

  ## 1. Designing the schematic - 1 hour
   I used KiCad to design the schematic, loosely following the guide on Blare.

I used Onshape to design the CAD, splitting the casing into 2 pieces, top and bottom, all connected by nuts and bolts, and keycaps for the switches and a knob for the rotary encoder, and a small hole for the USB-C port
